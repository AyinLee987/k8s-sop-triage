Pod-Scoped Network Delay: Diagnose Degradation Before Blaming Resources
For degradation with pods Running, endpoints intact, and no resource saturation, suspect pod-level network delay and confirm with pod-scoped RTT/latency probes or a latency spike localized to one workload while dependencies and control-plane remain normal.
Distinguishing evidence: slow network paths (high RTT, retransmits, timeouts) with normal CPU, memory, disk I/O, restart count, readiness, and endpoint membership; upstream/downstream traffic anomalies are usually secondary.
Rule out resource saturation, load/traffic surge, dependency/DB/queue latency, pod restarts, readiness/endpoint loss, DNS/network-policy errors, and application/GC pauses before concluding.
Correlate onset with network metrics and verify recovery when the delay is removed or the pod is relocated; never conclude from alert symptoms alone.
