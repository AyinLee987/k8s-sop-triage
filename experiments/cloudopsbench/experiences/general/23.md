Kubelet Unavailability Masquerading as Workload Pending
When availability drops and pods are Pending/unschedulable, check the target node’s status and kubelet service; if the node is Unknown with unreachable taints and kubelet is inactive/dead, classify as node-level kubelet failure.
Distinguish from scheduler/control-plane outage by healthy scheduler bindings and Ready control-plane; from resource pressure by False pressure conditions; from pod-level faults by PodScheduled=False and no image/probe/crash evidence.
Before concluding, rule out control-plane/scheduler failure, node resource pressure/eviction, network partition affecting only kubelet, and pod spec/resource constraints as the primary cause.
Confirm kubelet recovery returns the node to Ready and affected pods schedule or recover.
