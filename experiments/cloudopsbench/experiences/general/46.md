Pod-Level Network Delay Suspected When Healthy Endpoints Show Traffic Drop
When quality degrades but pods stay Ready with no restarts and endpoints remain populated, compare per-pod latency and inbound/outbound traffic; localized delay shows throughput/traffic drop without pod failure.
Distinguish from CPU/memory saturation, application exceptions, dependency/DB failures, DNS/endpoint churn, and node-wide network faults by checking resource metrics, error logs, dependency health, endpoint continuity, and node-level network.
Rule out global service slowness, downstream failure, and intentional traffic loss before concluding; confirm with per-pod RTT/latency or packet-delay evidence and recovery after pod relocation/restart.
