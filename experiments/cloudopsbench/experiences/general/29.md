Localized Pod Network Delay Diagnostic Principle
For service-quality degradation, suspect pod-level network delay when latency symptoms are confined to one pod/replica while sibling replicas, dependencies, and co-located workloads stay healthy and the pod remains Running without restarts.
Distinguishing evidence is pod-scoped response-time inflation with normal endpoint readiness, no restart/OOM, and no node-wide or cluster-wide resource saturation; downstream CPU or throttling alerts are secondary unless they align with onset and persist after isolating the affected pod.
Rule out application CPU/memory throttling, GC pauses, dependency/database latency or errors, DNS/endpoint failures, traffic spikes, and node/CNI-wide network faults before concluding pod network delay.
Confirm with pod-level network latency/path measurements and recovery after pod replacement or isolation; if multiple pods on the same node are affected, escalate to a node/CNI cause.
