Differential diagnosis: path-specific deterministic 5xx/disruption with healthy pods and valid endpoints
Application-code defect (bad return/logic in entry handler; swapped positional args or call-signature mismatch): confirms path-specific deterministic errors on valid input, low overall error ratio concentrated on one handler/action, error chain traced to internally generated bad status/logic, caller arg order/types mismatch callee contract, reproducible from code/request inputs, and disappears after correction; ruled out when no source/contract mismatch or reproduction is absent and failures are broad timeout/connection/restart/network/dependency-driven.
Pod crashes/OOM/resource pressure: confirms restarts, OOM, or resource exhaustion; ruled out by Ready workload, stable/no restarts, and normal capacity metrics.
Missing endpoints/service-endpoint-port misconfiguration: confirms empty/invalid endpoints or port mismatch; ruled out by populated valid endpoints and path-confined failures.
Saturation/quota limits: confirms saturation or quota exhaustion; ruled out by normal capacity metrics and no resource pressure.
Network/DNS faults: confirms connection failures, timeouts, or DNS errors; ruled out by normal connectivity/DNS and deterministic path errors rather than timeouts/connection failures.
Dependency outage/overload, downstream/database latency or failure: confirms downstream latency/failure/overload; ruled out by healthy dependencies and no dependency-driven failure pattern.
Deployment/config changes: confirms a correlated change explaining the failing path; ruled out when no deployment/config change explains it.
Check order (cheapest/most discriminating first): confirm Ready/no restarts, valid populated endpoints, and path-specific deterministic errors vs timeouts/connection/resource pattern; then check capacity/saturation/quota and network/DNS; then downstream/dependency health; then deployment/config changes; then inspect source and caller-callee contract and reproduce from request inputs; finally verify correction removes path-specific failures.
