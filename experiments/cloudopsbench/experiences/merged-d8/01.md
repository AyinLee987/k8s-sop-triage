Quota admission failure: missing replicas plus FailedCreate forbidden and saturated namespace quota

When expected pods are absent while sibling workloads run, inspect controller events first; downstream connection errors are symptoms, not proof of runtime failure.
Confirm a namespace ResourceQuota reports used requests/limits at the hard limit and pod creation is rejected as exceeded quota; this identifies admission rejection.
Distinguish from scheduling/node pressure, image pull/startup, crashloop/readiness, DNS/network, and endpoint failures by verifying affected pods are not merely Pending, restarting, unready, or unreachable.
Rule out quota misread/stale events, wrong namespace, and normal autoscaling churn; verify the rejected workload's resource requests would exceed the quota and that quota relief or lower requests restores creation.
Conclude only when pod absence, FailedCreate forbidden, saturated quota, and recovery after quota change align.
