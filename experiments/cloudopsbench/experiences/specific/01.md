Boutique availability disruption caused by namespace CPU quota exhaustion
Symptom: frontend logged connection refused to productcatalogservice :3550; GetResources showed productcatalogservice deployment 0/1 while other boutique pods were Running.
Cause: namespace/boutique ResourceQuota cpu-quota at requests.cpu 10/10; productcatalogservice ReplicaSet events showed FailedCreate "exceeded quota: cpu-quota, requested requests.cpu=1, used=10, limited=10".
Check first: kubectl get resourcequota cpu-quota -n boutique and kubectl get events -n boutique for FailedCreate/exceeded quota; also kubectl get deploy productcatalogservice -n boutique.
Fix: reduce CPU requests or raise quota, then verify productcatalogservice pod schedules and frontend :3550 connection-refused errors stop.
