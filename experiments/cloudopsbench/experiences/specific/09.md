currencyservice down — Deployment references missing ServiceAccount `currencyservices`

Symptom: frontend logs flood with `could not retrieve currencies: rpc error: code = Unavailable ... dial tcp <pod-ip>:7000: connect: connection refused`; checkout also fails on currency conversion.
Signal: `GetResources` pods shows no currencyservice pod; `GetResources` deploy shows `currencyservice 0/1` (0 updated, 1 unavailable) while everything else is Running.
Cause: `DescribeResource deploy/currencyservice` shows `Pod Template: Service Account: currencyservices` — that SA does not exist in `boutique`, so the ReplicaSet is rejected at admission and no pod is ever created.
Check first: `kubectl -n boutique describe deploy currencyservice | grep -i 'service account'` and `kubectl -n boutique get sa` — compare names before touching nodes, images, or ports.
Fix: create SA `currencyservices` (or correct `serviceAccountName`) and verify the deployment rolls out to 1/1 and frontend currency errors stop.
