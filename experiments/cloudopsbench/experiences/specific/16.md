Boutique partial unreachability — namespace service quota exhausted (Admission_Fault)
Reported frontend checkout failures; frontend logs showed DNS "lookup checkoutservice ... no such host" while checkoutservice pod was Running.
Cause: namespace_service_quota_exceeded on namespace/boutique — ResourceQuota service-quota was at services: 11/11, so the checkoutservice Service could not be created.
First checks: kubectl get resourcequota service-quota -n boutique, kubectl get svc -n boutique, kubectl get events -n boutique for quota/admission errors.
Mitigate by raising the service quota or deleting unused Services, then recreate the missing checkoutservice Service and verify frontend checkout.
