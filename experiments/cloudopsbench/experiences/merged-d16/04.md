Differential diagnosis: ClusterIP connection refused/timeouts with Running/Ready backends
Port mapping mismatch (targetPort/containerPort/listener, incl incorrect targetPort): confirms Running/Ready pod labels match selector but targetPort names an absent container port or a numeric port differs from the listener, Endpoints/EndpointSlice show no usable/wrong port, and correcting targetPort restores endpoint port/client connectivity; rules out when endpoint port matches declared/listening port and client path recovers unchanged.
Protocol/transport mismatch: confirms Service transport differs from app expectation (e.g., UDP Service for TCP/gRPC) and direct check to Service port fails while backends stay healthy; rules out when Service and probe/listener protocol match and direct check succeeds.
Selector mismatch: confirms Running/Ready pod labels do not match selector and endpoints are empty/misselected; rules out when labels match and endpoints list the pod.
Readiness failure: confirms pod Running but not Ready and endpoints omit it; rules out when pod is Ready and endpoint is ready.
Crashed pod: confirms pod not Running/restarting/crash and endpoints absent; rules out when pod is stably Running/Ready.
App not listening: confirms no process listener on declared/container port; rules out when listener is present on that port and direct pod check works.
DNS/name-resolution failure: confirms name resolution fails before ClusterIP; rules out when name resolves to ClusterIP and direct IP:port behaves the same.
NetworkPolicy/firewall: confirms drops after endpoint selection to Service/backend port; rules out when direct backend/Service path on that port succeeds or failure is config-level port/protocol.
Service type/exposure errors: confirms wrong type/exposure blocks the client path; rules out when ClusterIP/port path is correct and internal connectivity matches client symptom.
Empty/misselected endpoints: confirms Endpoints/EndpointSlice empty or ports unusable despite healthy pods; rules out when endpoints contain correct ready addresses/ports.
Check order: 1 compare Service port/protocol/targetPort with containerPort/listener and probe protocol (cheapest, catches mapping/protocol); 2 inspect endpoints/EndpointSlice addresses and ports (selector/readiness/crash/empty); 3 check pod Running/Ready/restarts; 4 test app listener and direct pod connectivity; 5 test DNS then direct ClusterIP/backend path (NetworkPolicy/firewall); 6 check Service type/exposure.
