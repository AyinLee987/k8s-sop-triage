Path-specific deterministic 5xx/disruption with healthy pods and populated endpoints

Application-code defect (bad return/handler logic/argument mismatch): confirms deterministic error on valid input traced to internally generated bad status/logic or caller/callee arg-order/type mismatch, with path-specific failures gone after correction; ruled out by broad timeout/connection/restart pattern, normal capacity/dependencies, or no reproducible code/input link.
Pod crash/OOM/resource pressure/readiness failure: confirms restarts, NotReady, OOM, or saturation; ruled out by Ready pods with stable restarts and normal resource/capacity metrics.
Service/endpoint/port misconfiguration: confirms empty/mismatched endpoints or selector/port error; ruled out by populated valid endpoints and working routes.
DNS/network/discovery/network-policy/ingress/quota-limit fault: confirms DNS/registration gap, connection failures, policy/ingress error, or quota limit; ruled out by normal DNS/network, no broad timeout/connection pattern, and no policy/quota finding.
Dependency/downstream/database outage, latency, or overload: confirms downstream errors, latency, failure, or overload; ruled out by healthy downstream/dependencies and no broad timeout/latency/capacity pattern.
Deployment/config change: confirms temporal correlation with rollout/config change; ruled out by no recent deployment/config change and a reproducible code/input cause.
Gateway/ingress route mismatch: confirms route-specific 404/NotFound while other routes work, failing path mismatches route definitions, and successful-vs-failing route comparison; ruled out by matching route, populated endpoints, and no backend 5xx/timeouts.

Check order: 1 pod readiness/restarts/resources; 2 service endpoints/selector/port; 3 gateway/ingress route definitions and access logs; 4 DNS/network/discovery/policy/ingress/quota; 5 downstream/database health/latency; 6 deployment/config changes; 7 application code/request-input reproduction and caller/callee contract.
