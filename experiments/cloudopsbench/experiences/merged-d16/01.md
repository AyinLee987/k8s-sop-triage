Title: Workload availability gap with missing pods or pending new instances and partial unreachability while siblings remain active
Quota admission rejection: confirm no pod object/replicas, FailedCreate forbidden/exceeded quota, namespace quota used requests/limits at hard limit, rejected requests would exceed quota, and relief/lower requests restores creation. Rule out if quota is below limit, events are stale/wrong namespace, churn is normal, or pods are merely Pending/restarting/unready/unreachable.
Missing or misspelled ServiceAccount: confirm 0 available/0 total, no pod object, and an admission event naming the absent ServiceAccount; downstream dial errors are secondary. Rule out if a pod object exists, the ServiceAccount exists correctly in the same namespace, or quota/limit-range/webhook/RBAC/spec/security-context/ConfigMap/Secret explains admission.
Quota-blocked dependent object/PVC creation: confirm Pending PodScheduled=False, quota at/over limit, referenced claim absent, no scheduling events, siblings Running, and raising quota/lowering demand lets the dependent object and pod create. Rule out if scheduling/storage events exist, the claim exists/binds, or image/runtime faults are present.
Scheduling capacity/taints/affinity: confirm Pending with scheduling events and no admission denial. Rule out when FailedCreate/quota/ServiceAccount evidence aligns and no scheduler events exist.
Storage/PV/StorageClass: confirm missing/unbound claim or storage events. Rule out when the dependent object was never created because admission was blocked and quota is at cap.
Image pull/startup/config rollout: confirm image-pull, startup, or config-rollout errors on an existing pod. Rule out when no pod object exists or admission denial precedes runtime.
Crashloop/readiness/pod health: confirm restarting/unready/unhealthy existing pods. Rule out when pods are absent/unadmitted and siblings remain Running/Ready.
DNS: confirm lookup failures/timeouts. Rule out when admission rejection aligns and DNS resolution works.
Service selector/endpoints: confirm empty or incorrect endpoints. Rule out when endpoints are normal or no pod exists due to admission.
CNI/network-policy: confirm CNI or policy faults with reachability gaps. Rule out when admission/quota evidence aligns and existing pods remain reachable.
Order: 1) inspect controller events for FailedCreate and whether any pod object exists, the cheapest and most discriminating check.
Order: 2) check namespace quota usage vs hard limit, rejected-request math, dependent object existence, and the relief test.
Order: 3) check ServiceAccount/existence and other admission blockers, then scheduling/storage, runtime/image/readiness, and DNS/endpoints/CNI/network-policy only after admission is excluded.
